This module adds **quick quizzes** across all lectures and **flashcards** with further likely questions.

## How to answer a theory question for full points

- **Define** first (formula if there is one): "p(r_k) = n_k / n, where…".
- Then the **reason / property** the question asks for ("…so the mean brightness is preserved").
- For "what are the steps" questions, give a **numbered list** with one line of purpose per step.
- For "where is it used" questions, name **two or three concrete places** from the lectures.

Use the flashcards actively: write your answer in the box **before** revealing the model answer, then rate yourself honestly. Cards marked "partly" or "didn't know" stay marked, so you can come back to them.
